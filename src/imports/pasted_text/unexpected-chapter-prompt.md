Design a breathtaking, emotionally cinematic, highly interactive personal memory website dedicated to a best friend. The website is a surprise wishing website that will be sent exactly at 12:00 AM.

The concept is not a normal birthday website. It should feel like entering a beautiful digital memory book, cinematic scrapbook, and interactive journey through an unexpected friendship.

PROJECT TITLE:
"The Unexpected Chapter"

TAGLINE:
"Some people enter your life unexpectedly and somehow become part of your story forever."

OVERALL EMOTIONAL CONCEPT:

Create a website that tells the complete story of two best friends through chronological memories, photographs, places, travels, happiness, arguments, emotional moments, and unforgettable experiences.

The friendship began unexpectedly. The two people experienced happiness at its peak and sorrow at its peak. They fight often, but they never truly ignore or leave each other. They travelled to many places together and created countless memories.

The entire website should feel deeply personal, warm, nostalgic, cinematic, romantic in friendship emotion but NOT like a couples or dating website.

DESIGN STYLE:

Create an ultra-premium, visually stunning, cinematic experience.

Use:

- Deep midnight navy and black backgrounds
- Warm cream and beige paper textures
- Soft golden lighting
- Subtle warm brown accents
- Vintage film grain
- Polaroid photographs
- Scrapbook paper elements
- Handwritten notes and annotations
- Cinematic gradients
- Soft glowing particles
- Subtle stars
- Elegant smooth animations
- Glassmorphism only where necessary
- Premium editorial typography
- Nostalgic analog photography feeling

The website should look like a combination of:

- A cinematic movie opening
- A luxury digital scrapbook
- A travel journal
- A personal diary
- A memory timeline

Avoid:

- Generic birthday templates
- Excessive balloons
- Cartoon designs
- Bright pink romantic couple aesthetics
- Too many colors
- Cheap-looking UI components

TYPOGRAPHY:

Use elegant serif typography for emotional headings similar to Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or Bodoni style.

Use a clean modern sans-serif for descriptions similar to Inter or Manrope.

Use handwritten typography for photo captions and personal notes.

The visual hierarchy should feel editorial and cinematic.
